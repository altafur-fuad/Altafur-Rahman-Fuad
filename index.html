Research

ACADEMIC RESEARCH

Research in Progress · 1 Ongoing Project

CROSSMAMBA-AGRI

Cross-Attention Vision Mamba for Joint Pest and Disease Classification

A unified Vision Mamba (State Space Model) architecture that jointly classifies plant diseases and insect pests in a single network. Incorporates a Background Suppression Module (BSM) for noisy field imagery, multi-scale patch embeddings, a shared bidirectional Mamba encoder, and task-specific cross-attention routing for resource-efficient diagnostic deployment. Supervised by Mohammad Salah Uddin Chowdhury, Assistant Professor & Chairman, CSE, BGC Trust University Bangladesh.

Disease Branch — PlantVillage (17 Classes) Pest Branch — IP102 (5 Classes) Architecture: 38.61M Parameters
