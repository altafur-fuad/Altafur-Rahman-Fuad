CROSSMAMBA-AGRI

CrossMamba-Agri: A Vision Mamba-Based Framework for Joint Crop Disease and Pest Classification

A dual-head Vision Mamba (State Space Model) framework designed for joint crop disease and insect pest classification on the multi-crop CCMT dataset (Cashew, Cassava, Maize, and Tomato). Incorporates a Background Suppression Module (BSM) combining 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) filtering with SimAM parameter-free 3D attention to suppress irregular field clutter, Multi-Scale Patch Embeddings (8×8, 16×16, and 32×32 patches mapped into 196 tokens, D = 512), an 8-layer Bidirectional Mamba (Bi-Mamba) encoder providing linear sequence complexity O(N), and Cross-Attention Fusion (12 adaptive queries, 8 attention heads) routing features to dual task-specific MLPs. Evaluated on an audited, 100% leakage-free parent-level split. Supervised by Mohammad Salah Uddin Chowdhury, Assistant Professor & Chairman, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, BGC Trust University Bangladesh.
